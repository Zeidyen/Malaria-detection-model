Malaria upsurge early detection, Ghana: prototype

District-month alerts from the final system (expected-cases model with satellite and climate layers, zone adjustment and a likely-cause check), 247 districts, January 2018 to December 2025.

Research prototype, supplementary to the paper. Retrospective: each month was predicted from data before it, but this page is not connected to DHIMS2 and is not an operational system. Alerts are statistical signals for review, not confirmed upsurges.

Zone-wide signal: observed / expected cases by ecological zone

Ratios well above 1 across a whole zone point to a widespread rise (or a reporting change), which the district alerts partly adjust away. Shown for review; no alert threshold is set for it in this prototype.

Methods: negative binomial INLA model, refitted monthly on all earlier data (season by zone, spatial district effect, district × year AR1, rainfall, land surface temperature, soil moisture, NDVI, ERA5-Land temperature and humidity with 0-3 month lags, land cover and night lights). Alert: observed above the upper 95% limit after the leave-one-out zone adjustment (about 1.6% of district-months). Likely cause: real transmission rise vs reporting change, judged from malaria's share of outpatient visits and test positivity against the same month of the previous three years. Data: DHIMS2 (Ghana Health Service), CHIRPS, MODIS, SMAP, ERA5-Land, VIIRS, Dynamic World, WorldPop.
